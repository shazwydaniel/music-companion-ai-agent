# Original genre demos

Six original AI-generated teaching songs requested for Riffin’s capstone demo. Lyrics and simplified accompaniments were generated for this app, with no third-party song text or recording transcription copied. Common chord patterns are stylistic examples, not rules that define a genre. These new materials are dedicated under CC0 1.0 to the extent rights apply; this is not a claim that every jurisdiction treats AI-generated material identically. The human-created mascot remains separately credited in asset-attribution.md.
